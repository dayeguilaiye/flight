# Flight Home Surface Brief

Scope: `frontend/src/features/home/pages/HomePage.tsx`. Mode is Persuade in service of a simple selection task; airy density, subtle motion, medium variance, light theme, and shared Flight accents.

Audience and job: a visitor arriving at Flight needs to understand that it is a small collection of independent experiments and choose a real tool immediately.

Content and constraints: one concise product statement, exactly two real tool destinations in the first release, full Chinese/English parity, semantic links, responsive access, and no shared application header or destination-page wrapper. Do not show future placeholders, fake proof, or long editorial filler.

Direction: a quiet editorial index rather than a card dashboard. The first viewport uses page-owned typography, rules, numbering, and generous space to make the LLM API tester and interactive music-theory learning feel like two distinct entries in the same carefully edited collection.

Unresolved decisions: none blocking implementation.
