# Music Theory Surface Brief

Scope: `frontend/src/features/music-theory/pages/MusicTheoryPage.tsx` and its feature-owned components. Mode is Operate with Read serving comprehension; comfortable density, subtle motion, medium variance, light theme, and the shared signal accent.

Audience and job: an adult beginner without a piano needs to turn unfamiliar theory terms into visible keys, structural roles, and audible relationships. The primary task is to open any lesson directly, manipulate its example, and hear the result. No progress history or prerequisite is required.

Content and constraints: six deep chapters, a free laboratory, full Chinese/English parity, local Web Audio synthesis, no backend, no feature persistence, no gamification, and complete keyboard/mobile access.

Chosen direction: vertical chapter deep-dive, approved comp `.impeccable/mocks/decision/music-theory-chapter-dive.png`. The memorable moment is selecting a chord or structure in the lesson and seeing the docked piano immediately label, highlight, and play the exact participating notes.

Unresolved decisions: none blocking implementation.
